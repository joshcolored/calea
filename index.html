Our Story

Born from Love & Sugar

Calea Pastries & Coffee was born from the heart of a mother and her love for her daughters — Carissa and Leanne. Owner Ging Consing named the café by blending their names, and that warmth has been baked into every cake ever since.

What began as a small, beloved bakeshop along Lacson Street has grown into one of Bacolod City's most iconic institutions. Locals swear by it. Tourists plan entire trips around it. First-timers in Bacolod are sent straight here.

Today, with branches across the city, Calea remains rooted in its original promise: real cakes, honestly made — thick with filling, rich in flavor, and priced so everyone can indulge.
